Need help? Email support@swipephotos.app — we usually reply within two business days.

How do I review photos?

Pick a stack on the Library tab (Random, On This Day, a month, or Similar). Swipe left to mark a photo for deletion, right to keep it. When you finish, review the Trash and tap Delete. iOS asks you to confirm.

Can I get deleted photos back?

Yes. Deleted photos move to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Why does the app need access to my photo library?

To show your photos and delete the ones you choose. Everything happens on your iPhone; your photos are never uploaded. If you chose “Limit Access”, the app only sees the photos you selected — tap the banner on the Library tab to choose more.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Swipe Photos Pro › Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Swipe Photos Pro in the app’s Settings tab. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, open Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.
