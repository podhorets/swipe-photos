Privacy Policy

Swipe Photos · Last updated November 1, 2026

Swipe Photos (“the app”) helps you review and delete photos on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: your photos never leave your device, the app has no accounts, and the limited technical data it collects is used only to keep the app working and improve it.

Your photos

With your permission, the app reads your photo library to show your photos, group similar shots and help you choose what to delete. All of this happens on your iPhone. Photos, thumbnails and photo metadata are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting a photo uses Apple’s Photos framework: iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted photos stay in the Photos app’s Recently Deleted album for 30 days.

Your review progress (which photos you kept, settings, streaks) is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Data we collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos. The app uses these services, which receive limited technical data:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.)Crash reports and diagnostics: app version, device model, iOS version, error details. If the app crashes, a short screen recording of the moments before the crash, with all images and text masked.Finding and fixing crashes.
PostHog, Inc.Anonymous usage events (for example “review started”, “paywall shown”) with a random identifier.Understanding which features are used so we can improve them.
RevenueCat, Inc. and AppleSubscription purchase and status information with an anonymous identifier. Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.Providing and restoring your Swipe Photos Pro subscription.

This data is not linked to your identity, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or tracking across other apps or websites. You can turn off usage analytics and crash reports at any time in the app under Settings › Share Analytics.

Retention

Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days and usage events for up to 12 months, after which they are deleted or aggregated. Subscription records are kept as long as required to provide the subscription and meet legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

  • Change photo access at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.
  • Turn off analytics and crash reports in the app’s Settings.
  • Delete all locally stored app data by deleting the app.
  • Request access to or deletion of any data associated with your anonymous identifiers by emailing us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
